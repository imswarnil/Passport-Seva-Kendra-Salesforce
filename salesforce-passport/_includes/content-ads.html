{%- comment -%} Renders lesson content with in-article ads injected automatically. {% include content-ads.html content=content %} How the split works: kramdown ends every TOP-LEVEL paragraph with the exact sequence "

\n\n" — a closing tag followed by a blank line. Paragraphs nested inside a blockquote or list item are indented, so their closing tag is followed by "\n" or "\n" instead. Splitting on "

\n\n" therefore only ever lands us at a top-level block boundary, and an ad can never end up inside a blockquote, a list or a table. Splitting on the paragraph END (rather than on paragraph-to-paragraph adjacency) matters: most paragraphs here are followed by a heading, a table or a list, so keying on adjacency would count almost nothing in a long lesson. An ad goes in after every Nth paragraph, repeating, and never within two blocks of the end. Lessons shorter than `min_paragraphs` get none at all. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign body = include.content -%} {%- assign n = site.adsense.in_article.every_n_paragraphs | default: 7 -%} {%- assign floor = site.adsense.in_article.min_paragraphs | default: 12 -%} {%- capture sep %}
